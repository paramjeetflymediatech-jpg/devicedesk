3. Step-by-Step Execution Reference Table

Stage Role Platform Area Actions & API Payload
01. Service Booking Corporate Client /portal/client/book-service Selects retainer, fills detailed brief, attaches files & Google Drive folder URL (POST /api/client-services/requests).
02. Management Triage Admin / Management /admin (Client Queue) Evaluates project requirements, checks team capacity, assigns designated Team Leader (assigned_tl_id).
03. Task Delegation Team Leader (TL) /portal/leader/client-requests Selects active reporting specialist, adds technical guidelines and execution notes (POST /api/tasks with project_id).
04. Member Execution Specialist (Dev / Design / SEO) /employee-dashboard Accepts task on Kanban, works under Desktop Screenshot Agent, attaches proofs (GitHub/Figma/Drive), and enters completion note.
05. Quality Audit Team Leader (TL) /portal/leader (Modal) Side-by-side verification: Matches original client brief (Before) against employee deliverables (After). Rejects or marks Completed.
06. Client Delivery Corporate Client /portal/client Green 'Work Delivered & Completed' card appears. Client downloads verified assets and can chat directly with TL for questions.
